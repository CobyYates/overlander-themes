What's Inside

Nine tools.
One platform.

We built every piece because we needed it ourselves. From the first idea on the forum to the final journal entry — Expeditionist holds the whole arc of a trip.

01 / Garage

Your rig, documented.

Build sheet, spec sheet, maintenance log, and trip history. Every mod, every mile.

02 / Route Planner

Map it. Then drive it.

Plot waypoints, label camps, mark hazards. Stage a multi-day route. Export GPX or share the link.

03 / Calendar

Trail rides & meetups.

Find the next group run, host one yourself, or filter by region, club, or vehicle requirement.

04 / Groups & Roles

Squad up, organized.

Build a group. Assign roles — Trip Leader, Tail Gunner, Medical, Comms. Show up knowing your job.

05 / Packing Lists

Never forget the boards.

Smart packing lists scale with the trip — duration, terrain, weather, group size. Save templates.

06 / Trip Journals

The run, written down.

Log miles, photos, camp, conditions. Pin trail-condition reports for the next driver.

07 / Forums

Talk shop with overlanders.

Build threads, trail reports, gear takes. Regional sub-forums. No algorithm, no sponsored bait.

08 / Gear Library

Compare specs. Skip hype.

How It Works

Plan it. Pack it.
Drive it. Log it.

From a half-formed idea on the forum to a journal entry your future self will thank you for — here's how a trip moves through Expeditionist.

01

Plan

Browse the calendar for an event, or build your own route with mapped waypoints, stages, and alternates.

02

Pack

Smart packing lists scale to weather, terrain, and trip length. Assign trip roles to your group.

03

Drive

Live tracking, group messaging, role-based comms. Your route, your packing list, your group — in your pocket.

04

Log

Pull route, photos, and notes into a clean trip journal. Drop a trail-condition report for the next driver.
